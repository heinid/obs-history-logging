# History Logging — era systems

Each `##` is one era SYSTEM (a civilization's complete chronology). Under it,
one line per era: `<year> <name>`, where the year is where that era begins
(bare = AD, or add `BC`). An era runs until the next line's year. Pick a system
from the timeline's era dropdown to lay it over the shared BC/AD axis — it only
renames the groups, it does not hide any notes. Edit here by hand or via
**Manage era systems** (main-pane button or command palette).
